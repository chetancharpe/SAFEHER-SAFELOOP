# Privacy

SAFELOOP collects only data needed for the safety flow.

Location:

- Used for route comparison, Safe Journey, SOS location sharing, and responder distance ranking.

Microphone:

- Used only in the browser while the Safe Journey page is active.
- The app does not claim unrestricted background microphone access.
- The demo trigger is clearly labeled and does not fake a speech-recognition result.

Trusted contacts:

- Stored so users can configure emergency contacts.
- Demo notifications are labeled **DEMO NOTIFICATION**.

Data controls:

- `DELETE MY DATA` removes journeys, SOS events, contacts, notifications, feedback, and safety scores.
- `DELETE MY ACCOUNT` removes the account.
